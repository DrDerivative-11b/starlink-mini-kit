Based on a real working build. This is the exact kit used by people who already have a Starlink Mini. The case holds the dish (mounted in the lid), power station, solar panel, and cables so everything stays protected and ready to go.

The Starlink Mini itself is not included — this is the supporting gear.

1

Apache 4800 Weatherproof Case Harbor Freight

IP65 hard case with pick-and-pull foam. Large enough for the Starlink Mini (mounted in the lid), Anker power station, solar panel, and cables. ~$60 at Harbor Freight — much cheaper than Pelican equivalents.

~$60 Buy at Harbor Freight Amazon options
Comes with foam. You can customize the foam or leave the pick-and-pull as-is. The beige/tan version is shown in the original build photos.
2

Starlink Mini Lid Mount (Inside Case) Etsy

3D-printed mount that holds the Starlink Mini securely in the lid of the Apache 4800 so it doesn’t fall when the case is closed or carried. Specific model for the 10 3/16" × 11 3/4" Mini.

~$45 Buy on Etsy
Seller notes: lightly sand the inner lid surface so the adhesive/glue sticks well. Multiple people have confirmed it fits the Apache 4800 perfectly.
3

Anker SOLIX C300 DC Portable Power Station

288Wh LiFePO4 battery. Powers the Starlink Mini for many hours and can be recharged from solar, USB-C, or a car. Compact enough to fit in the case with the rest of the gear. Multiple USB-C ports + car socket.

~$200 Buy on Amazon
The DC version (no AC outlets) is lighter and cheaper. Runtime on a Mini is typically 7–9+ hours depending on usage. Pair with solar for longer trips.
4

Foldable Solar Panel (60–100W)

Recharges the Anker power station off-grid. A 60W or 100W foldable panel fits in the case and keeps the system running indefinitely in good sun. Anker SOLIX panels are the easiest match; other compatible panels also work.

$80–200 Anker 60W options 100W options
The original build uses a foldable black panel that packs into the case. Confirm XT60 or compatible connector for the C300 DC.
5

Cables & Small Accessories

USB-C cables (for charging the power station and powering the Mini), a car charger adapter if you want to top up while driving, and any extra connectors. Most of these come with the power station or are cheap add-ons.

~$20–40 USB-C cables
The original kit includes coiled cables and a small car charger. Keep a spare USB-C to barrel or official Starlink cable as backup.

Assembly Tips (from the original build)

  • The case comes with pick-and-pull (breakaway) foam — pull out what you need so everything tucks in securely.
  • Mount the Starlink Mini in the lid using the Etsy mount so it doesn’t fall when the case is closed or carried.
  • Lightly sand the inner lid surface where the mount attaches so the glue/adhesive sticks well.
  • The solar panel and power station sit in the bottom half of the case with the cables tucked around them.

About Official Starlink Batteries

Starlink does not currently sell an official portable battery pack for the Mini. Aftermarket clip-on packs (such as the PeakDo LinkPower 2) offer ~4–6 hours of runtime and attach directly to the dish. Larger solutions like the Star-Batt exist but are much heavier and more expensive. The Anker + solar approach in this kit is one of the most popular DIY methods for longer off-grid use.
